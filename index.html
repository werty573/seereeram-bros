It started with transport and contract work in the Caroni cane fields. Ninety-three years later the fourth generation runs quarries, asphalt plants and a fleet of over a thousand units, with branch offices in Tobago and Guyana.

First in the world to take delivery of the Caterpillar AP1055F asphalt paver.

First local contractor to introduce slurry sealing to the roads of the Caribbean.

100% local engineers, superintendents, surveyors and technicians.

Several thousand kilometres of highways and secondary roads built or rehabilitated, and 90+ miles of highway maintained in Guyana.
